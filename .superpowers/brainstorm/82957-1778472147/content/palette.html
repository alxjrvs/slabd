Color palette — three lanes inside "Vintage Modernized"

All three pass WCAG AA at 17px body (older-seller floor). Type stays Oswald + IBM Plex. The differences are paper temperature, accent saturation, and how loud the brand voice gets.

VERIFIED · GRADED · INSURED
Sample body copy at 15px on cream. Sufficient contrast for extended reading. Older-eye-friendly without leaning medical.
★ Featured CGC 9.8

A · Newsprint Classic

Paper: Cream #F5EDD8 · Ink: Navy #1A2A4A · Accent: Brick #B23A48 + Sun #FFD23F
Feels like: 1970s Marvel cover stock + modern restraint. Most "comics-coded" of the three.
Trade-off: Cream paper limits photography pop — comic covers (which are loud already) compete with the bg.
Best if: You want the marketplace to feel like a comic shop, not a fintech.

SLABD
VERIFIED · GRADED · INSURED
Sample body copy at 15px on warm-white. Higher base contrast, calmer mood. Lets cover photography do the work.
★ Featured CGC 9.8

B · Catalog Index

Paper: Warm white #F7F4ED · Ink: Deep #0F1B2D · Accent: Vermilion #C1432F + Muted Gold #D9C77A
Feels like: Library card catalog × modern auction house. Quieter than A, lets covers pop.
Trade-off: Less personality. Reads "respectable" more than "fun."
Best if: You want photography (comic covers) to carry the visual energy and the chrome to step back.

SLABD
VERIFIED · GRADED · INSURED
Sample body copy at 15px on near-black. Maximum contrast, premium-cinema mood. Photography reads like a gallery wall.
★ Featured CGC 9.8

C · Pulp Noir

Paper: Near-black #16161A · Ink: Paper #F2E9C9 · Accent: Blood #E63946 + Amber #FFC857
Feels like: Mike Mignola / Black Hammer / Criterion noir. Highest cinematic / premium signal.
Trade-off: Older sellers can read dark mode (WCAG passes) but some find inverted UI fatiguing. Solvable with a "light mode" toggle that uses palette B.
Best if: Default-dark is part of the brand statement and the swipe deck should feel like a black-box gallery.

Accessibility floor: all three palettes hit WCAG AA at 17px body. The "older sellers" constraint actually argues against C as a default (light mode is easier on aging eyes), but C works beautifully as a default for the consumer-buyer side with light fallback for the seller dashboard. A/B → consider as system-wide; C → consider as default with light toggle.
