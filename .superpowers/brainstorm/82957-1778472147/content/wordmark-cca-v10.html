Center S — ben-day dots

Halftone screen applied to the center "S" — a regular grid of white knockouts through solid ink, evoking the mid-century four-color comic press. At large sizes (marketing, app icon) the dot pattern reads as a screened mid-tone. At small sizes (80px and below) the dots get small enough to blend back into solid ink — graceful degradation, the S stays legible.

Construction: The S is rendered twice — first with a ben-day fill (ink rect background, white dot foreground, 2.2-unit grid with 0.55-unit radius dots), then again with a thin ink stroke (0.5 width) over the top to keep the silhouette crisp at any size. The dot pattern naturally rasterizes into a screened mid-tone at marketing scale and into nearly-solid ink at thumbnail sizes — same trick a real comic press relied on.
